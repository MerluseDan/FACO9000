## Inference and boundaries

The scientifically useful delta is the joint cost/latency/error boundary for acquiring dependence information when ordinary reports are already present. The same observation history can support multiple hidden lineages; a correctly modeled audit can refine uncertainty while an overtrusted audit can worsen it. The frozen grid measures those tradeoffs rather than counting additional paths. The exact conventional policy prevents a gain from extra information from being misattributed to a portal architecture.

The broader previous candidate's temporal-drift, delayed-gold and empirical components remain open; they are not claimed completed by this narrow experiment. All findings, including an empty benefit region, will be retained.
