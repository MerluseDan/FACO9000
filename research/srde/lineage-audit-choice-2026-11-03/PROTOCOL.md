# SRDE-LA1: choosing a lineage audit versus another assertion

Frozen 3 November 2026 before scientific scoring. Concept/research direction: Dan Merluse; protocol, implementation and audits are AI-assisted. CPU/stdlib experiment; no GPU, paid service, live agent or native SRDE modification.

Question: When is a fallible, delayed lineage audit worth its cost relative to another assertion, and how does audit-model error reverse that decision?

This is a dated narrowing of the previously unexecuted lineage-audit candidate. A three-slot single-case problem isolates information value. Temporal drift, delayed gold learning and worker scheduling are deferred. A method is given model probabilities; this is not learning hidden source reliability. Distinctness is a documented extension of the SRDE benchmark, not a new Bayesian algorithm or certified first-ever experiment.

Y is an actual binary latent event with prior .5. L is a latent copy indicator, independent of Y. Three initial reports have marginal accuracy .8. If L=1, the first two are copies of one draw; otherwise all three are independent given Y. The third is always independent. A maximum of two new assertions have accuracy .7 and cost .015 each. One audit reports L with assumed accuracy q. It does not report Y. All costs are artificial decision-loss units, not monetary expenditures.

At times 0,1,2 choose stop, fresh assertion, one audit, or wait for a pending audit. Every non-stop action consumes one slot. Responses whose arrival time is at or before the next decision are delivered first. Audit delay 0 becomes usable at the next slot, delay 2 after two slots, and delay 4 misses the horizon. At time 3 a terminal forecast is required. Stop terminates immediately and does not later receive pending evidence. The primary loss is squared probability error, or .15 if abstaining, plus all incurred acquisition costs. The terminal rule minimizes posterior expected loss; abstain iff p(1-p)>.15. Abstained p is still retained for full Brier scoring.

The exact declared grid has 36 correctly specified conditions: copy prior {.2,.8}, audit quality {.5,.8,1}, delay {0,2,4}, and audit/new-assertion cost ratio {.25,4}. Four quality shifts assume q=1 but generate q=.5 or 0, across both copy priors. Two prior shifts assume copy prior .5 but generate .1 or .9. All 42 conditions are fixed before outcomes. Scores are uniformly averaged over conditions within each family; no deployment-frequency interpretation is supplied.

Policies: StopBayes; NaiveIndependent (incorrectly fixes L=0); exact conventional FreshOnlyDP; AuditFirst (forces audit at slot 0 then optimizes remaining fresh/wait decisions); GreedyVOI (one-step expected loss); SRDE_AuditRule (audit the two-agree/one-dissents pattern if q>=.8, timely and no more costly than a fresh assertion, otherwise use FreshOnlyDP); conventional GraphFullDP via state-graph backward induction; PortalFullDP via recursive Bellman expansion; OracleLineageDP with free actual L, explicitly an information-advantaged diagnostic. All non-oracle methods share initial observations, query slots, costs and potential outcomes. Recursive/bottom-up equality is an expected control, not architecture superiority.

Primary estimand: exact expected total decision loss of GraphFullDP minus FreshOnlyDP averaged over the 36 specified conditions. The comparison isolates the audit option. Heuristic, blanket-audit, one-step, misspecification and calibration contrasts are secondary. Exact finite expectations have no sampling confidence interval. Separately generate 100 untouched cases per seed and condition for seeds 8401–8405, giving 21,000 cases/189,000 policy forecasts; use 400 paired independent-case bootstrap draws for specified-grid loss contrasts. These intervals condition on the authored grid and ignore generator/model-choice uncertainty.

Enumerate every positive-probability atom (Y,L,initial reports,two potential assertions,audit response), and retain raw weights and individual forecasts. The separately written random sampler uses the same declared distribution, not an independent empirical dataset. The selector receives observable state and assumed model only; the environment reveals observations. Gold Y and L are not passed to non-oracle selectors. Impossible model evidence falls back to p=.5/abstention, with a recorded flag. No fitting, model selection or parameter tuning uses test outcomes.

Verification: fourteen pre-scoring structural/analytic unit checks; independent output auditor without importing the runner; complete second-process replay; exact JSON and decompressed CSV comparison excluding runtime. Controls include useless and late audits, budget/latency guards, label symmetry, probability mass, per-state recursive/iterative equality and shifted audit reliability. Freeze runner, tests, JSON/Markdown protocol and novelty record by SHA-256 before scoring. A scientific code change requires a dated revision and retained failure; no silent outcome-driven repair.

Limits: exact small known-model decision theory, authored binary reports, no natural language, no learned grounding, no dynamic state learning, no empirical independent dataset, no biological/affective claims and no actual-world predictive validation. The implementation cannot establish a new mathematical advantage for recursion.
