## Integrity and interpretation

Each published directory has a `GITHUB_FILES_SHA256.txt` for this exact expanded publication. Historical manifests/receipts are retained, and each `COMPLETE_PACKAGE.zip` is the exact previously delivered archive. `COMPLETE_PACKAGE.zip.sha256` checks that filename; any original archive checksum file retains its original delivered filename.

P1 and P2 were recovered from their existing authenticated Drive archives. Their existing GitHub text files differed only in terminal whitespace; the complete uploaded snapshots restore their original source bytes and add omitted files. No scientific result or historical preregistration was rewritten. Newly uploaded completeness is not a new preregistration.

The binary study's public-data pilot rerun reproduces the original Portal/Katz scores. The learned-temporal study's local replay is exact; five non-HGB methods also match across Colab/local execution. HGB differs across scikit-learn versions, and the failed all-model tolerance check remains visible. Do not describe that entire comparison as exact reproduction.

The test packages contain the individual observations and predictions needed to check reported metrics. They are not proof of human cognitive theory, natural affect semantics, future-event forecasting outside their tested domains, or native engine integration. Historical E30 references in the reports are separate evidence; E28's primary package remains unrecovered and no fabricated E28 score is supplied.

The delayed-feedback study is now complete; its negative results and full evidence are linked below. Other active research branches and existing preregistrations are preserved; results are not pooled across independently designed studies.
