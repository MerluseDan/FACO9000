## Integrity and interpretation

Each published directory has a `GITHUB_FILES_SHA256.txt` for this exact expanded publication. Historical manifests/receipts are retained, and each `COMPLETE_PACKAGE.zip` is the exact previously delivered archive. `COMPLETE_PACKAGE.zip.sha256` checks that filename; any original archive checksum file retains its original delivered filename.

P1 and P2 were recovered from their existing authenticated Drive archives. Their existing GitHub text files differed only in terminal whitespace; the complete uploaded snapshots restore their original source bytes and add omitted files. No scientific result or historical preregistration was rewritten. Newly uploaded completeness is not a new preregistration.

The binary study's public-data pilot rerun reproduces the original Portal/Katz scores. The learned-temporal study's local replay is exact; five non-HGB methods also match across Colab/local execution. HGB differs across scikit-learn versions, and the failed all-model tolerance check remains visible. Do not describe that entire comparison as exact reproduction.

The test packages contain the individual observations and predictions needed to check reported metrics. They are not proof of human cognitive theory, natural affect semantics, future-event forecasting outside their tested domains, or native engine integration. Historical E30 references in the reports are separate evidence; E28's primary package remains unrecovered and no fabricated E28 score is supplied.

The delayed-feedback study is now complete; its negative results and full evidence are linked below. Other active research branches and existing preregistrations are preserved; results are not pooled across independently designed studies.

## LA1 — lineage audit choice, completed 3 November 2026

[Full report and reproducibility package](lineage-audit-choice-2026-11-03/RESEARCH_REPORT.md). Cross-chat and prior-research checks precede the public freeze. Exact finite expectations plus 21,000 held-out cases test when a costly, fallible, delayed lineage query is worth more than another assertion. The optimal policy purchases audits in only 2/36 specified settings; the average benefit is small, the sampled interval includes zero, and overtrusted audits reverse it. Recursive and conventional policies tie. [Private UpdateInbox folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1s4wmPhpFUWiL4wL7O6_RfYM3GbPe1e4t). This is a documented benchmark extension, not certified worldwide novelty or native SRDE integration.


## LA2 — delayed verifier learning, completed 3 November 2026

[Detailed report](la2-verifier-learning-2026-11-03/REPORT.md), [PDF](la2-verifier-learning-2026-11-03/REPORT.pdf), and [complete 33-file source/data reproduction archive](la2-verifier-learning-2026-11-03/SRDE_LA2_Verifier_Learning_2026-11-03.zip). Across 160 calibration worlds, typed feedback reduces verifier-reliability error, but does not repay acquisition cost at the primary 512-case horizon relative to fresh evidence. Twelve tests and exact two-process replay pass. [Verified private UpdateInbox copy](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Mf4AWEmwXaGzOKq8bsolvOK6ZiHxXjLk). Established observer learning/verification-bias methods; documented SRDE extension, no worldwide-first or cognitive-validation claim.

## LA4 — grounding versus provenance audits, completed 8 November 2026

[Detailed report](la4-grounding-audit-2026-11-08/REPORT.md), [PDF](la4-grounding-audit-2026-11-08/REPORT.pdf), [observability construction](la4-grounding-audit-2026-11-08/OBSERVABILITY_PROOF.md), and [complete 33-file source/data archive](la4-grounding-audit-2026-11-08/SRDE_LA4_Grounding_Audit_2026-11-08.zip). All 557,568 forecasts and 7,472 query receipts independently audited; 12 tests and 11-file exact scientific replay pass. [Private canonical UpdateInbox folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jmthmovsrWStj8HfXMzMXBaVm6YUEeh0).
