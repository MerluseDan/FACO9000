# SRDE research instructions

Before freezing or launching a new discovery experiment, apply novelty-audit-2026-11-03/NOVELTY_GATE.md. Read the closest primary studies, compare the internal experiment register, document a substantive and falsifiable difference, and record unresolved literature gaps. New seeds, thresholds, names, recursion depths or equivalent recursive/iterative implementations alone are not sufficient novelty. Label replications and controls honestly. A candidate marked NOT CLEARED must have its gaps resolved and a documented extension rationale before it becomes a new discovery study. Preserve all earlier protocols and negative results. A bounded search never proves first-ever status. Refresh the literature for each new protocol and before publication.
