# Prior-work/internal-overlap record — LA4
Reviewed8 November2026 before scientific freeze. Status: controlled SRDE grounding/observability extension, not a new theorem or active-learning algorithm.

LA3 learned source accuracy/dependence within a model family. LA4 addresses a specific structural blind spot: evidence distributions and provenance can remain identical while their relationship to the event reverses by context. It compares the information content and cost of asking about lineage versus independently anchored event outcomes, with abstention and short lookahead. The scientific reason is to distinguish model fit from semantic/target identification and parameter accuracy from action utility.
